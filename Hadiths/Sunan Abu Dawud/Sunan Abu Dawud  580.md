## Arabic

## Bengali

## English

Narrated Uqbah ibn Amir: I heard the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) say: He who leads the people in prayer, and he does so at the right time, will receive, as well as those who are led (in prayer) will get (the reward). He who delays (prayer) from the appointed time will be responsible (for this delay) and not those who are led in prayer

## French

Rapporté par Uqbah ibn Amir رضي الله عنه : J’ai entendu le Messager d’Allah ﷺ dire : « Celui qui dirige la prière à l’heure prescrite recevra la récompense, et ceux qui prient derrière lui aussi. Celui qui retarde la prière en dehors de son temps en sera responsable, et non ceux qui prient derrière lui. »

## Indonesian
