## Arabic

وَحَدَّثَنَاهُ عَبْدُ بْنُ حُمَيْدٍ، أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، بِهَذَا الإِسْنَادِ جَاءَ أَفْلَحُ أَخُو أَبِي الْقُعَيْسِ يَسْتَأْذِنُ عَلَيْهَا ‏.‏ بِنَحْوِ حَدِيثِهِمْ وَفِيهِ ‏ "‏ فَإِنَّهُ عَمُّكِ تَرِبَتْ يَمِينُكِ ‏"‏ ‏.‏ وَكَانَ أَبُو الْقُعَيْسِ زَوْجَ الْمَرْأَةِ الَّتِي أَرْضَعَتْ عَائِشَةَ ‏.‏

## Bengali

## English

This hadith has been narrated on the authority of Zuhri with the same chain of transmitters (that 'A'isha said):There came Aflah, the brother of Abu'l Qulais (Allah be pleased with him), and sought permission from her, the rest of the hadith is the same (except for the words that the Holy Prophet) said:" He is your uncle. Let your hand be besmeared with dust. Abu'l Qulais was the husband of the woman who had suckled 'A'isha (Allah be pleased with her)

## French

Rapporté par Aïcha رضي الله عنها : Aflah, le frère d’Abu’l Qulais رضي الله عنه, est venu demander la permission d’entrer chez elle. Le reste du hadith est identique, sauf que le Prophète ﷺ a dit : « C’est ton oncle. Que ta main soit couverte de poussière. » Abu’l Qulais était le mari de la femme qui avait allaité Aïcha رضي الله عنها
