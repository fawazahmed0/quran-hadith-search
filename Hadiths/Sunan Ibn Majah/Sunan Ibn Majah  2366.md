## Bengali

। আমর ইবনে শুআইব (রাঃ) থেকে পর্যায়ক্রমে তার পিতা ও তার দাদার সূত্রে বর্ণিত। তিনি বলেন, রাসূলুল্লাহ সাল্লাল্লাহু আলাইহি ওয়াসাল্লাম বলেছেনঃ প্রতারক (খেয়ানতকারী) নারী-পুরুষ, ইসলামী আইনের আওতায় হদ্দের শাস্তি ভোগকারী এবং বিপক্ষের প্রতি শ্রত্রুতা পোষণকারীর সাক্ষ্য গ্রহণযোগ্য নয়।

## English

It was narrated from 'Amr bin Shuaib from his father that his grandfather said:“The Testimony of a man or woman who is treacherous, or of one who has been subjected to one of the Haad punishments of Islam, or of one who bears a grudge against his brother, is not permissible.”

## French

Rapporté par 'Amr bin Shuaib, d’après son père, de la part de son grand-père : “Le témoignage d’un homme ou d’une femme qui est traître, ou de quelqu’un qui a subi une des peines prévues par l’islam, ou de quelqu’un qui a de la rancune envers son frère, n’est pas accepté.”

## Turkish

Amr bin Şuayb'ın dedesi (İbn Amr) (r.a.)'den rivayet edildiğine göre; Resulullah (Sallallahu Aleyhi ve Sellem) şöyle buyurdu, demiştir: «Hâin erkeğin, hâin kadının, İslâmiyet'te had cezasına çarptırılmış olan kimsenin şâhidiiği ve kin ile husûmet sahibinin, husûmet beslediği kişi aleyhinde şâhidlik etmesi caiz değildir.» Not: Zevaid'de şöyle denilmiştir: Bunun senedinde Haccac bin Ertat vardır. Bu ravi tedIis ederdi. Bunu da an'ane ile rivayet etmiştir. Tirmizi bu hadisi Aişe (r.anha)'dan rivayet etmiştir. Bu hadis Zevaid türünden sayılmıştır. Halbuki Ebu Davud da, bunu az bir değişiklikle rivayet etmiştir. EBU DAVUD HADİSİ VE İZAH İÇİN TIKLA
