## Arabic

## English

It was narrated that Ibn ‘Abbas said:“The people were going in all directions, and the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: ‘No one should depart until the last thing he does is (Tawaf around) the House.’”

## French

Rapporté par Ibn ‘Abbas : Les gens partaient dans toutes les directions, et le Messager d’Allah (ﷺ) a dit : “Que personne ne parte avant que la dernière chose qu’il fasse soit de tourner autour de la Maison (Tawaf).”
